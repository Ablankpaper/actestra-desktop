## Last material defect and fix

The packaged recovery smoke exposed `event-mismatch`. In real Electron
parent-port delivery, a successful worker response could arrive before the
operation's `task.started`, tool, and terminal event messages. Tests that
delivered messages in one turn had hidden the race.

The General Worker now sends every successful operation's ordered event batch
before its success response. The response is therefore the delivery barrier.
The affected two root files pass 10 tests with messages deliberately delivered
on separate turns.
